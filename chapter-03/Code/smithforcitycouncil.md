# Meet John Smith  
*Candidate for Columbus City Council*
  
  ## Who is John Smith?
  
  John Smith is a dad, a coach, and a proud neighbor in Columbus, Ohio. He grew up on the south side of the city and now lives in Linden with his wife and two kids. John went to Columbus City Schools and later earned a degree in public service from Ohio State University.

John has worked for over 15 years helping local families through his job with a community nonprofit. He also coaches youth soccer and volunteers at the local food bank.

## What Has He Done?

- Helped open a new community center in his neighborhood  
- Led clean-up events in city parks  
- Worked to bring more job training to local high school students  
- Spoke at city meetings to support safer streets and better housing ...
